Cat Tap Hunt: Kitty Game (developed by P.V. Nimantha Madhushan & P.V. Ruwantha Madhushan) is a playful hunting simulation designed to entertain and engage your cat. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. By playing Cat Tap Hunt, you agree to the practices described below.

📋 1. Information We Collect

Cat Tap Hunt is designed with minimal data collection. We only access what's needed to provide the game and optional video recording features:

🎥 Video Recordings Optional gameplay clips saved locally on your device — never uploaded to our servers. You can delete any recording at any time, and recordings are never accessible to us or any third party.
📸 Thumbnails Generated preview images stored locally alongside your videos for easy browsing.
⚙️ Game Settings Preferences like character size, speed, count, and auto-delete rules — stored locally via Hive.
📊 Scores & Stats Local game scores and session history — kept only on your device.

🔹 We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or precise location. No account creation is required.

📲 2. Device Permissions Required

  • Camera & Microphone — completely optional. Only requested when you tap "Enable Recording." The game works fully without them.
  • Storage / Files — to save and manage recorded video clips and thumbnails on your device.
  • Vibration — to provide haptic feedback when your cat catches prey (can be disabled in settings).
  • Internet — not required for core gameplay; only used if you choose to share videos via external apps.

You can revoke any permission at any time from your device settings. Disabling camera/mic will turn off recording but the game will still work perfectly.

⚙️ 3. Third-Party Services & Technologies

Cat Tap Hunt uses the following trusted technologies:

Hive (local storage) — stores game settings, scores, and video metadata locally on your device. No cloud sync. video_thumbnail — generates thumbnail previews of recorded clips entirely on-device. Share Plus — enables sharing your cat's hunting videos to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or other apps you choose. Flame Engine — powers the smooth animations and gameplay; no data collection.

🔹 No advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or data brokers are integrated. Your cat's privacy (and yours) is fully respected.

🎯 4. How We Use Your Information

  • ✓ Provide the core hunting game experience for your cat.
  • ✓ Optionally record and store gameplay videos locally for you to enjoy and share.
  • ✓ Remember your custom settings (speed, size, prey count, auto-delete preferences).
  • ✓ Track scores locally so you can see your cat's progress.
  • ✓ We do not sell, rent, or trade any data — there's simply nothing to sell.

🔒 5. Data Storage & Security

Your data stays on your device. Here's how we keep it safe:

  • Local-only storage: All videos, thumbnails, scores, and settings are stored inside the app's private directory using Hive. Other apps cannot access them.
  • No cloud uploads: We do not transmit your videos, gameplay data, or any personal information to external servers.
  • You control deletion: Delete individual clips, entire sessions, or all recordings at any time from the Video tab. Auto-delete can also be enabled (1–30 days retention).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data permanently.

👥 6. User Rights & Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your recordings and settings are visible directly in the app.
  • Deletion: Delete any video, session, or all recordings instantly from the Video screen.
  • Auto-delete: Set a retention period (1–30 days) to automatically remove old clips.
  • Permission control: Enable/disable camera, mic, or vibration anytime in your device settings or the app's Settings tab.

🧸 7. Children's Privacy

Cat Tap Hunt is designed for cat owners of all ages. The app's content is appropriate for young children (ages 5 and up), but the app is intended to be set up and managed by adult cat owners.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because Cat Tap Hunt does not collect any personal information from any user, we do not collect personal information from children under 13.

The app does not require any personal data to function. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us — though we likely have none to delete.

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect new features or legal requirements. When we do, we'll update the "Effective Date" at the top. For significant changes, we may add an in-app notice. Your continued use of Cat Tap Hunt after changes means you accept the updated policy.
